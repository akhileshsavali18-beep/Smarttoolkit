Privacy Policy for APS TOOLS

Effective Date: September 2026 • Maintained by Akhilesh Systems

1. Offline First & Zero Remote Storage

APS TOOLS is built as an offline-first application. All processing (including photo compression, PDF document generation, and mathematical calculations) occurs directly on your physical Android device. We never transfer, store, or view your photos, documents, or data on external cloud servers.

2. Device Permissions

The app requests limited Android device permissions solely to deliver features you invoke:

  • Storage / Media: Required solely to pick images from your gallery for compression/PDF creation and save output results to your local device.
  • Sensors (Accelerometer / Magnetometer): Used locally in real time for the Compass and Bubble Level tools. No sensor data is ever recorded.

3. Advertising & Analytics

The application integrates standard Google AdMob SDKs to display rewarded and contextual advertisements, keeping the 50+ tools free. Google may collect anonymized advertising identifiers in accordance with the standard Google Play Services privacy framework.
